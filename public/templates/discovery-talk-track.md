# Discovery Talk Track — Manufacturer First Meeting (45–60 min)

**Goal:** Qualify exclusive / limited-network intent, map buying committee, earn a solution-design working session.  
**Not the goal:** Feature dump. Price discussion. Premature RFP volunteerism.

---

## Opening (3 min)

“Thanks for the time. We’re Vanscoy Rare — independent rare pharmacy out of Pittsburgh. Same leadership DNA that built rare pharmacy at PANTHERx and patient services at RareMed. We’re here to learn how you’re thinking about U.S. distribution for [ASSET], and whether an exclusive or tightly limited model is on the table.”

---

## Context questions (10–12 min)

1. Where is [ASSET] in the U.S. timeline (filing, PDUFA, launch)?
2. How are you defining success for patients in the first 90 days post-launch?
3. What does “good” look like for HCPs and for your brand team?
4. Have you already decided exclusive vs. limited vs. open — or is that still open?

**Listen for:** undecided network design, lean commercial team, fear of PBM conflict, prior SP disappointment.

---

## Access & operations (10 min)

5. Pharmacy benefit, medical benefit, or mixed?
6. Cold chain / packout / REMS / specialty handling requirements?
7. Expected U.S. patient band Year 1 and peak (order of magnitude is enough)?
8. Will hub / patient services sit with the SP, a separate hub, or hybrid?

---

## Buying process (8 min)

9. Who owns network selection? Who can say no?
10. What does the evaluation path look like (sole source, RFI, RFP)?
11. Decision timing vs. launch / PDUFA?
12. Which other pharmacies or hubs are already in conversation?

---

## VRP positioning (8 min) — only after discovery

Use one proof point, not five:

- Independence + rare-only focus  
- Executive-led program design  
- Integrated SP + patient-services mindset  
- Live exclusives: Redemplo® (plozasiran / FCS); Cycle VENXXIVA  
- TranSParency™ / vIMPACT data posture  

Close the block with: “If useful, we can run a disease-specific working session with clinical + access + data — no proposal theater.”

---

## Close / next step (5 min)

**Always leave with a dated next step.** Examples:

- Working session with clinical + market access (date)  
- Intro to economic buyer (name + date)  
- NDA + redacted KPI pack (date)  
- Explicit “not a fit / revisit at [milestone]”

**CRM exit for S1:** Economic buyer identified, network intent known, next step dated.

---

## Disqualify fast if…

- Network will be broad/open with PBM SPs mandated and no independent seat  
- Decision already signed with incumbent and no transition appetite  
- Evaluation is fee-only with no clinical/data weighting  
- Asset is not rare / specialty-appropriate for VRP model
